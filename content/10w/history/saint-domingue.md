* Former colony of Saint Domingue had Americas' strongest export economy
    * Represented culmination of European colonization
    * By late 1780s, had become main destination of Atlantic slave trade
* Saint Domingue society was made up of different tiers
    * Enslaved population worked on different types of plantations
    * Free people of color made up middle class with broad range of people
