title: Haitian Revolution
tags:

  - revolution-age
---

* Slave uprising that happened between 1791 and 1803
    * Transformed French colony of [Saint Domingue](saint-domingue) into state of Haiti
    * Was the largest uprising to occur in the Americas
* Event of global significance because of uniqueness
    * Revolution reshaped world commodity markets and politics
* Historians disagree if it would have occurred without [French Revolution](french-revolution)
    * Destabilization from French Revolution may have been initiator
* Pursuit of freedom, equality and independence by slaves, free coloreds, whites
* Began as pursuit for self-government and free trade by white colonizers
    * Could not have succeeded without separate pursuits by other groups
* Revolution led to establishing [Louverture Constitution](louverture-constitution) in 1801
