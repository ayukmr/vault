* Resistance happened not just aboard ships, also when under threat of capture
* Built fortified towns, transformed habitats, burned down European factories
* At least 61 attacks on ships by land-based Africans in 17th and 18th centuries
* Employed variety of resistive strategies that led to rising costs for slavers
