* Time period from 1500s to 1700s
* Many separate empires that were interconnected by trade
* Many different goods were traded in the Colombian Exchange
    * Crops, silver sourced from Americas moved to Europe
    * Animals, diseases sourced from Europe moved to Americas
* [Indian ocean trade](indian-ocean-trade) consisted of major trade routes
