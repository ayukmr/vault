title: Industrial Time
tags:
  - history-10
  - industrialization
---

* Before, time was vague, approximate concept
    * Labor was task-oriented rather than time-based
    * Time related to length of tasks
* Industrial Revolution created concept of hourly wages
* Workers were reluctant to accept the new time discipline
    * Workers often showed up late or were absent
    * Many observed Saint Monday, tradition of Monday abseentism
* In new economy, time became indistinguishable from money
