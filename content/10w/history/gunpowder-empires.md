* During 16th century, three land-based empires used gunpowder to grow
    * Safavid Empire: 1501-1736
    * Mughal Empire: 1526-1857
    * Ottoman Empire: 1299-1922
* Peaked in 16th century as they used military to access wealth of Arabian sea network
* All embraced Islam (Ottomans were Sunni, Safavids were Shiite, Mughals tolerant of all)
* Similar political and economic structures
    * Sons ascending throne, ruled through bureaucracy
    * Reliant on productive agriculture with surplus to tax
