* Claimed that England had advantages in many areas
    * Geographic location
    * Moral advantages and just legal system
    * General peace and liberty
    * Hardworking people
* Different forms of nationalism
    * Civic nationalism where defined by ideals
    * Can also be based on birthplace or heritage
* Countries were pressured to open trade
* [Tokyo Conference](tokyo-conference) was held to oppose British rule
    * [Mexico](mexican-nationalism) participated in conference
