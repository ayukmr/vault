* By late 18th century, Europe annually consumed:
    * 13 million pounds chocolate
    * 120 million pounds coffee
    * 125 million pounds tobacco
    * 500 million pounds sugar
* Elites and lower class both consumed produced goods in different forms
* Tea, tobacco, sugar, coffee were objects of mass consumption
    * 25% of adult population may have used them daily
* [Import substitution](europe-import-substitution) allowed for innovation among European producers
