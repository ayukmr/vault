---
title: Napoleon Bonaparte
tags:
  - history-10
  - revolution-age
---

* Wins support and recognizes Catholic church
* In 1802, became consul for life
* In 1804, became Emperor and began return to "Roman Empire"
* Established the Napoleonic Code
    * Largely abolished differences in rank, power
    * Eliminated restrictions on Jews, increased them on women
* Created police state with strict censorship
* Restored monarchical system with hierarchies and titles
