* _Renaissance_ literally means "rebirth"
* Began in Italy, due to money and being comprised of city states
* Long distance trade allowed for investing with high returns
* In 1517, [Martin Luther](martin-luther) challenged Catholic church
* General population declines, crises known as [crisis of the seventeenth century](seventeenth-crisis)
* Jean-Baptiste-Colbert (1619-1683) wrote [_Instructions_ for finance](seventeenth-accounting)
