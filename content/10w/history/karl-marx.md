* Born to middle-class Jewish family in Germany
* Completed PhD in philosophy, became publishing in radical journals
    * Was exiled from German states
* Met socialists in France, was exiled again
* Organized with Communist League, wrote [_Communist Manifesto_](communist-manifesto) in 1848
* Was exiled three more times, ended up in Britain
* From Britain, developed his theories in _Capital_ in 1867
