---
title: Marx's Humanity Stages
tags:
  - history-10
---

* Primitive communism
    * Early hunter, gatherers with no private property
* Slave society
    * Greece and Rome are key examples
    * Slaves are forced to produce goods for masters
    * Slave owners controlled means of production
    * Growing divide between free citizens and slaves
* Feudalism
    * Land is primary means of production
    * Hierarchical relationship between lords and serfs
* Capitalism
    * Rise of industrialization
    * Means of production is privately owned by capitalists
    * Illiterate workers sell labor to survive
* Socialism/communism
    * Proletariat overthrow bourgeoisie and establish socialist society
    * Means of production is collectively owned
    * Would evolve into communism in classless, stateless society
