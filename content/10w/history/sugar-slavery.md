title: Sugar and Atlantic Slavery
tags:

---

* Sugar was a luxury good only consumed by the wealthy
* Used as a sculpting ingredient to form elaborate food art known as "subtleties"
* Demand for sugar drove colonization, wars, slavery in Caribbean
* Triangular system of trade between England, Africa, Caribbean for sugar
    * Guns, cloth, iron are exported from England to Africa for slaves
    * Slaves are exported from Africa to Caribbean for cheap labor
    * Sugar is exported from Caribbean to England for profits
