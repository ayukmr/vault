---

* Took Russia out of WW1 "and Europe" to create communist state
* Impact was as large as French Revolution in terms of consequences
* New communist ideology was anticapitalist and atheistic
    * Western governments feared and distrusted it
    * US refused to recognize new Russian government until 1933
* Politically, Russia in 1900 had similarities to France in 1780
* Tsar Alexander II (1855-1881) launched liberalizing reforms
    * Most importantly, emancipated serfs in 1861
    * Caused widespread social and economic changes in Russia
* Certain groups believed future was more tied with West or East
