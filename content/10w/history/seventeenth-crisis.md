title: Crisis of the Seventeenth Century
tags:
  - history-10
  - early-modern
---

* Crisis was linked with cooling known as Little Ice Age
    * Climate conditions caused crop yields to drastically lower
* Three main reasons for inflation in Europe
    * Demographic expansion in Eurasia in sixteenth century
    * Reliance on cash and debasement of currency
    * Spanish conquest of New World bringing precious metals
* Only rulers recognizing cooling temperatures were Mughals ruling India
* Thirty-Years' War (1618-1648) contained nearly all European nations in war
* Wars defined emerging European state system
    * Wars involved virtually all European states in single system
    * Wars led to consolidation into fewer political units
* Taxes and loans supplied money to rulers of European states
* Rulers would rather use "divine right" to resources than force
* European states imposed duties on virtually all imported goods
* France lost Seven Years' War (1756-1763), lost claims in North America and India
* As empires reached limits of expansion, rulers had to legitimize rule
