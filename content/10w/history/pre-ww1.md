* Some time before 1914, Europe became destabilized
    * Before, believed they would create a prosperous civilization
* After 1870, European nations were fearful of others in Europe
* Germany formed Triple Alliance with Austria-Hungary and Italy
    * Additionally signed reinsurance treaty with Russia
* German treaty with Russia lapsed after Bismarck's retirement
    * In 1894, Franco-Russia treaty was signed
    * Contrasting French and Russian ideologies were disregarded
* Europe was divided into German-Austrian-Italian and Franco-Russian
* Naval competition between Germany and Great Britain arose
    * By 1912, German navy was significant force
    * Both countries had navy to protect colonies and secure trade
* In 1902, British allied with Japan against Russia
* Ententes were had between Britain, France, Russia
    * Older Triple Alliance faced new, looser Triple Entente
* The Balkans faced disruption as independent groups emerged
* In 1914, Bosnian revolutionary assasinated Archduke Francis Ferdinand
    * Francis Ferdinand would have become emperor of Austria
    * Austrian government was determined to stop South Slav separation
* Serbs rejected Austrian ultimatum to preserve sovereignty
    * In response, Austria declared war on Serbia
    * Russia prepared to defend Serbia, deployed troops
    * Germany demanded end to mobilization, declared war on Russia
    * Germany additionally declared war on France due to their allyship with Russia
    * Finally, Britain declared war on Germany as allies with France
* Most backward nations drew more advanced nations into war
