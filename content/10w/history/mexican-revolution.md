* Mexico was Spain's most valuable colony
* Made up of 18% whites, 18% natives
* Church held great power in Mexico
* Fall of the Spanish crown sparked revolt
    * Many economic factors increased the revolt
    * Followers had different ideologies
    * Certain beliefs lacked support from upper-class
* Mexico was unstable after the revolution
    * Over half a million people died
    * City was decaying with no money available
* On 19 May 1822, Iturbide named himself Emperor
    * Wanted congress that favored class and occupation
    * Led to being representative of population
* Differences between creoles and peninsulares
    * Resented each other but were also confused for each other
