title: Brazilian Revolution
tags:

---

* Until 1815, was a colony under Portugal
    * Brazil was forced to solely export goods to Portugal
* In 1808, King Joao fled to Brazil because of French invasion into Portugal
* With King Joao in Brazil, began to gain more political power
    * Eventually got equal status with Portugal in 1815
* As Brazil was gaining political power, Portugal was losing power
* Joao was forced to leave Brazil and return to Portugal
    * Took all the money from the Brazilian treasury with him
    * In Portugal, Liberal Revolution creates constitutional monarchy
    * Portuguese Courts revoked number of laws in Brazil
* In September 1822, Pedro, son of Joao, declares Brazilian independence
    * Led to war in which Portuguese were defeated
    * In December 1822, Pedro declares himself as emperor of Brazil
