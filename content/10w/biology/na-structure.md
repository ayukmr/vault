* Sugar is either ribose or deoxyribose
* Carbons in sugar are labeled from 1' to 5'
    * Base is attached to 1' carbon
    * Phosphate is attached to 5' carbon
    * Next phosphate is attached to 3' carbon
        * Can only add to the 3' carbon
        * Dehydration synthesis creates phosphodiester linkages
    * One chain has 5' at top, other has 3' at top

```
   \
 5' |   _O_
    | ,'   `.
    |'       `.
 4' \         / 1'
     \       /
      \_____/
   3'         2'
```

* Base A links to T, G links to C
    * A, G are purine
    * C, T are pyrmidine
    * Purine has two rings, pyrimidine has one

```
 5'      3'
 | A - T |
 |———————|
 | G - C |
 |———————|
 | T - A |
 |———————|
 | C - G |
 3'      5'
```
