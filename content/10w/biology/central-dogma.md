* Once information is in protein, cannot be retrieved


Can do this:
```
           ┌───────────┐
           │           ▼
DNA ────▶︎ RNA ────▶︎ Protein
 │                     ▲
 └─────────────────────┘
```

But not this:
```
 ┌────────┐ ┌──────────┐
 ▼        │ ▼          │
DNA       RNA       Protein
 ▲                     │
 └─────────────────────┘
```
