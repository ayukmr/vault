* Monomers are glycerol and fatty acids
* Creates different polymers
    * 1 glycerol, 3 fatty acids creates triglyceride
    * 1 glycerol, 2 fatty acids creates diglyceride
    * 1 glycerol, 1 fatty acid creates monoglyceride
    * 1 glycerol, 2 fatty acids, phosphate creates [phospholipid](phospholipids)
* Can be saturated or unsaturated with hydrogen
    * Unsaturated fats are bent
    * Saturated fats experience stronger LDFs
* Steroids
    * Cholestorol
        * Creates gaps, allows for movement
