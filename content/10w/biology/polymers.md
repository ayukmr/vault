---
title: Polymers
tags:
  - biology-10
---

* Made up of many monomers, individual molecules
* Held together by intramolecular forces
* Dehydration synthesis is adding monomer and releasing water
    * C bonded with H and C bonded with OH remove $H_2O$ and bond
* Hydrolosis reaction is adding water and releasing monomer
    * $H_2O$ can be added to C bonded to C
* Polymers create four basic types of [macromolecules](macromolecules)
