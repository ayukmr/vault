* Monomers
    * Glucose
    * Often hexagonal
    * [Monosaccharides](monosaccharides)
* Polymers
    * Starch
    * Multiple hexagonal
    * Polysaccharides
* Types
    * Chitin is strongest
    * Cellulose
        * Cheapest to make
        * Most common on earth
    * Peptidoglycan
        * Peptido (protein) and glycan (carb)
        * Flexible, similar to chain link
    * Glycogen is easily digestable
* Plants
    * Sun creates energy for creating glucose
        * $CO_2 (air) + H_2O (water) \rightarrow C_6H_{12}O_6 (glucose)$
        * Used for growing plant mass (biomass)
        * Used for creating ATP using mitochondria to sustain plant
        * Most glucose goes towards ATP production (90%)
* Humans cannot create glucose because of lack of photosynthesis
