title: DNA Replication
tags:
  - biology-10
---

* Happens during Synthesis (S) phase of cycle
* Human cells contain 46 chromosomes (2 of each kind)
    * Chromosomes get duplicated and become 92 chromatids
* DNA strands are split by proteins, creating forks
    * Single strands are replicated back into double strands
    * Replication happens in both directions
* Strands are split into leading and lagging strands
    * Leading strand is duplicated by simply adding nucleotides
        * Strand is oriented 3' to 5', so added from 5' to 3' since bases match
    * Lagging strand requires creation of Okazaki fragments, since it goes from 5' to 3'
        * Strand has to be created from 5' to 3', so have to create fragments
