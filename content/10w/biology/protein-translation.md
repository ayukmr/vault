* Creates [protein](proteins) from RNA

* Initiation
    * mRNA binds to small subunit of ribosome
        * Ribosome binding of mRNA binds to complementary sequence
    * Aminoacyl tRNA binds to start codon of RNA
    * Large subunit of ribosome binds, allowing translation
        * Has E, P, A sites
        * Acceptor, Performer, Exit
* Elongation
    * Extends polypeptide chain by moving through sections
    * tRNA moves to A site, anticodon base pairs with mRNA codon
    * Amino acid in P site is transferred to amino acid of tRNA in A site
    * Codon shifts back using elongation factors
* Termination
    * Terminates when stop codon is reached
    * Release factor binds to stop codon
    * Ribosome subunits separate from each other
