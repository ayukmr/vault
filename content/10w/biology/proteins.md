* Monomers are amino acids
    * Have variable r-group in center
        * 20 different variable groups
    * Have n-terminus and c-terminus on sides
    * Connected with peptide bonds using dehydration synthesis
    * Aminoacyl-tRNA synthesase has binding site for pair
        * Specifically for certain amino acid and tRNA
* Polymers are peptides
* Specific types for use cases
    * Hemoglobin
        * Red color in blood cells
    * Transcription factors
    * RNA Polymerase
    * Keratin, collagen
* Translated from RNA using tRNA
    * Translated using ribosome
        * Small subunit holds mRNA
        * Large subunit is site of peptide bond formation
        * Comprised of structural proteins and ribosomal RNA
* [Folded](protein-folding) for different roles
* Can have mutations that affect structure
    * Silent
        * Change in nucleic acid but not amino acids
    * Missense
        * Change in amino acid but with similar characteristics
    * Nonsense mutation
        * Adding a stop codon in an incorrect position
    * Frameshift mutation
        * Addition or removal of a single nucleic acid
        * Causes sets of three bases to be moved over
