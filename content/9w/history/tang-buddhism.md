tags:

---

* Remained most popular faith despite Daoism was officially highest religion
* Became a fundamental part of political order and economy
* Primary instruments were [imperial monasteries](imperial-monasteries) and palace chapels
* Apart from single attempted suppression, no emperor questioned place of Buddhism
* Along with ceremonies, Buddhist order assisted in defending empire
* Common people regularly encountered Buddhism in great festivals
* Influenced Tang life through popular lectures in large cities and small towns
* Central Asia scholars served as intellectual masters because of India origins
* In worldview, India held pride of center while China was in marginal position
* For grasping truth, India emerged as most direct and vivid medium
* In eighth century, China emerged as sacred Buddhist land in own right
* Another version of China center prophecy was used by [Empress Wu](empress-wu-prophecy)
