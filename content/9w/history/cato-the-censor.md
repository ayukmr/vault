---
title: Cato the Censor
tags:
  - history-9
  - ancient-rome
---

* Served as military man, provincial governor, senator and consul
* Despite antihellenism, was thoroughly familiar with Greek culture
* Mentions Themistocles and Pericles and was familiar with Greek books
* Despised members of Roman aristocracy who went too far in emulation of Greeks
    * An example was A. Postumius Albinus, consul in 151
* Despised the Greeks and was deeply mistrustful of all things Greek
