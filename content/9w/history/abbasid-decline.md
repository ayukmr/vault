---
title: Abbasid Dynasty Decline
tags:
  - history-9
  - islam
---

* Found tax base inadequate to support huge army and complex civil service
* Ex-soldiers seeking better salaries recognized different caliphs
    * The caliphs fought for power in savage civil wars
* For military support, many new rulers turned to independent military commanders
    * Many mamluks gained renown and some became rulers themselves
* In 945, caliphate fell under control of mountain warriors from Daylam
    * Abassid caliph remained, but Buyid princes controlled him
* In 1055, Turkish Seljuk family established Muslim state based on nomadic power
* In 1258, Abbasid caliphate in Baghdad was destroyed by Mongol invasions
