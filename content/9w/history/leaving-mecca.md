title: Muhammad Leaving Mecca
tags:
  - history-9
  - islam
---

* After failing to silence him, Quraysh arranged boycott
    * Boycott was devastating to the Companions
    * After a few months, boycott was lifted
* Tragedy struck in form of nearly simultaneous deaths of uncle and wife
    * Could no longer rely on uncle's protection
    * New Shaykh of Banu Hashim loathed him personally
    * Was openely abused on the streets of Mecca
* With loss of physical and emotional support, could no longer stay in Mecca
    * Answer came in form of invitation from small clan called _Khazraj_
* Fearing that he was leaving to raise army, men were chosen to kill him
