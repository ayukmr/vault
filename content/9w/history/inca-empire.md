---
title: Inca Empire
tags:
  - history-9
  - americas
---

* By 1525, had population of more than 6 million people
* Originated in Cuzco Valley, expanded militarily in mid-15th century
* Used traditional Andean social customs and economic practices
* Empire built on _mita_ labor, forced service to ruler
* Prosperity and military strength depended on llamas and alpacas
* As empire expanded, generally left local rulers in place
* On November 16, 1532, [first encounter](cajamarca-battle) between Inca and Spanish
