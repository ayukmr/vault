* Kharijite beliefs of state's rulers interfered with trade
* Led to development of first regular trade across Sahara desert
* Northern traders would trade salt for gold with southern nomads
* Earliest known beneficiary of exchange was Kingdom of Ghana
    * Appears in Arabic text of late eighth century as "land of gold"
    * North African city-states lost independence after Fatimid dynasty
    * Fatimids culminated rise to power by conquering Egypt in 969
* Fatimid dynasty fell in 1171 due to enemies from Europe
