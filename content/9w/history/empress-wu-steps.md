---
title: Empress Wu's Steps
tags:
  - history-9
  - empress-wu
---

* Came to the palace as concubine in 636 at age of twelve
* Attained position of power by:
    * Engineering escape from nunnery
    * Returning to palace under Gaozong's protection
    * Removing Empress Wang and concubine
    * Promoting family members to positions of power
    * Establishing herself as her husband's equal
* By 666, was permitted to make offerings to the gods beside Gaozong
* Arranged a series of murders, starting in 666
    * In 666, poisoning of teenage niece who attracted Gaozong's admiring
    * In 674, suspicious demise of her able eldest son, Li Hong
    * In 693, executed fourth son's wife and chief consort
* After Gaozong's death in 683, remained the power behind throne
* Manipulated succession of sons until 690, then taking power herself
* In 705, when more than 80 years old, was finally overthrown by another son
