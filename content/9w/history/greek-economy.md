* Slaves appeared in art but did not have thoughts recorded
* Work of slaves did not always take place in oikos, large numbers were employed in craft industries
* Aristotle wrote that "most men gain their livelihood from the land and its fruits"
* Most trade went by boat, land traffic being slow and expensive
* Diversity of natural resources in ancient world made trade a necessity
* Athenian commerce was driven largely by need for grain to feed large population
* Many rich people in Athens did not own land, as it was illegal to do so without an exception
