---
title: Women in Mongol Religion
tags:
  - history-9
  - mongols
---

* Religious system was based on duality of gods as heaven and earth
    * Second god was personified as female and bears epithet _eke_, "mother"
* Chinggis Khan performed rituals in honor of female ancestor Alan Qo'a
* Rituals were performed through shamans as intermediaries between deities and people
* Women engaging with shamanic rituals accused of being sorcerers and witches
    * Used by Muslim and Christian chroniclers to discredit political capability
* Some women accompanying Mongol campaign to Iran were Christian
    * Adapted tents into churches for priests, monks, Mongol _khatuns_
