title: Muhammad's Reforms
tags:
  - history-9
  - islam
---

* Muhammad united pre-Islamic positions of authority unto himself
* Anyone could join community by conforming to [first pillar](shahadah-pillar)
* Had almost unlimited capacity for growth through conversion
* Muhammad's primary function was to ensure protection of community members
* Equalized blood-worth so that no life was more valuable
* Instituted mandatory tithe called _zakat_ which every member had to pay
* Strove to attain some level of equality and independence for women
* [Women](islam-women) had specific role in society and were under special regulation
* Allowed slavery but forbade enslaving Muslims, Jews, Christians, Zoroastrians
* Companions were living repositories of the [hadith](hadith), giving information
    * _Hadith_ were the oral anecdotes recalling words and deeds of Muhammad
* Implemented [Constitution of Medina](medina-constitution), including rules for Jewish and tribes
