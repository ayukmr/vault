* Dissolution of caliphate multiplied centers of learning
* Islamic renaissance occurred throughout Islamic world
* Particularly dazzling in capital cities such as [Cordoba](middle-ages-cordoba)
    * Library contained largest collections of books in Europe at the time
* Many scholars read, translated, commented on works of ancient philosophers
* Others worked on astronomy and wrote on mathematics matters
* Newly independent Islamic rulers supported science and math
* Ibn Sina wrote books on logic, natural sciences and physics
