tags:

---

* Grew powerful on what then was considered the developed world
* Had more freedom compared to central states
* Did not have wealthy, entrenched nobility to contend with
    * Because of this, it was quicker to centralize power than rivals
* Buildup of Qin power began in 361 BCE with arrival of [Lord Shang](lord-shang)
* [King Zheng](king-zheng) was the most responsible for Qin's monumental task:
    * Mobilizing growing economic resources to conquer and pacify the Warring States
* Two major contenders to succeed the Qin were Xiang Yu and [Liu Bang](liu-bang)
