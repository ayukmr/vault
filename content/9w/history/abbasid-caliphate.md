tags:

  - islam
---

* Civil war in 750 ousted Umayyads and raised them to caliphate
* Found support in coalition of Shi'ites and non-Arabs excluded from Umayyad
* With new regime, Islamic center shifted from Damascus to Baghdad
* Administration grew more and more centralized in Baghdad
* Caliph Harun al-Rashid presided over flourishing empire from 786-809
* Had exotic imports from India and China such as tigers, panthers and silk
* Abbasid dynasty [began to decline](abbasid-decline) after caliph Harun's death
    * Dynamic [growth in outlying provinces](islamic-province-growth) paralleled gradual loss
