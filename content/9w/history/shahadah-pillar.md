* Acceptance of reality of God and prophecy of Muhammad
* In Arabic, _Shadaha_ means "to testify" or "to bear witness"
* Act of bearing witness to God's unity is most basic act of Muslims
* Briefest form is saying "There is no god but God" and "Muhammad is God's messenger"
* Traditionally, father whispers the Shadadah into a child's ear at birth
