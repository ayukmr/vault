* Was born in Nazareth in turbulent period of Jewish history
* Roman policies collided with hope for Jewish restoration theology
    * Message was brought that current order would succumb to heaven
    * Many Jews looked for signs of coming redemption
* Received John's message in preparation for coming Kingdom
* Continued to preach message of the Kingdom even after John's arrest
* The Kingdom he proclaimed would bring radical changes
    * Penitent tax collectors, harlots enter before chief priests, elders
    * Poor would precede rich, lowly would inherit the Land
* All felt bonded together in new community, called by knowledge of End
* Alarmed by possibility of use of Roman force, arranged to arrest him
