## Early (500-1000)
* Period happened after the fall of Rome
* Expansion of Islam, eventually stopped by proto French
* Provincial governments fought with each other

## High (1000-1300)
* Many different crusades happened
* Increased power of empire and the pope
* Arab technology/ideas spread to Europe
* Schism between East and West Church

## Late (1300-1492)
* New technology, especially military tech
* Large fall in crusade activity
* [Increased battles](seventeenth-crisis) between European powers
* Rise of the Ottomans as [Ottoman Empire](ottoman-empire)
* Further increase in [Italian commerce](renaissance)
