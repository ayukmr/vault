* His uncle Abu Talib kept him out of debt and was his protector
    * Uncle was the Shaykh, Arab leader, of Banu Hashim
* Widely known throughout the city as having virtue
* Things changed when attracting attention of [Khadija](khadija) and marrying her
    * Paved the way for acceptance at highest levels of society
    * Was extremely successful running his wife's business
* For fifteen years, struggled with differences in his lifestyle and beliefs
* Had a presence instruct him to recite a certain verse
    * Sources on first experience of Revelation are vague
* Waraqa told Khadija that "he is a prophet of this people"
* A second verse was sent assuring that he was now the Messenger of God
* [Left Mecca](leaving-mecca) after nearly simultaneous deaths of uncle and wife
* Divide in Islam religion [after death](after-muhammad) in 632 due to unclear caliph
