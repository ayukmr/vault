* Located off of Swahili Coast in present-day Tanzania
* When he visited, was quickly developing into center of trade
* In late thirteenth century, Mahdali dynasty took over island
  * By conquering nearby ports, had monopoly over gold trade

## Account
* One of the finest and most substantially built towns
  * Buildings made of wood, roofed with reeds, rains are frequent
* People engage in _jihad_, righteous struggle for religion
  * For the most part, people are religious and upright
* Sultan was Abu'l-Muzzafar Hasan, also known as Abu'l-Mawahib
  * Gave multitude of gifts and acts of generosity
  * Engaged frequently in raids of land of Zinj people
  * Man of great humility, sits and eats with poor brethren
  * Greatly respects men of religion and noble descent
