---
title: Empress Lu
tags:
  - history-9
  - qin-and-han
---

* When her son died in 188 BCE, placed infant on throne so she could hold power
* When that young emperor died, replaced with another infant
* Held power from 188 BCE to her death in 180 BCE through this
* Was a ruthless woman who had long been condemned by Confucian historians
* Was a competent ruler who provided stable leadership
* Once she died, many relatives were killed or dismissed and power was restored to Liu family
