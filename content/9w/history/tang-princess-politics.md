* History of court was battle between Taiping princess, Empress Wei, Anle Princess
* Coup in 710 in which Li Longji invaded palace and killed Empress Wei and her daughter
* Taiping Princess carelessly allowed Li Longji to gather Tang loyalists, her power waned
* After years of struggle and coup in 713 failed, her suicide brought end to female power
