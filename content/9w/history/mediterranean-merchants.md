* Long distance international trade was significant feature
    * Where there was trade, there was often piracy
    * Merchants turned into pirates, changing identities
* Transition from merchant to pirate was seen as commonplace
    * Could be seen as a form of business activity
* Cities dispatched fleets to prey on each other's trade
* Law of marque allowed for reimbursement on unjustly seized goods
