---
title: Roman Constitution
tags:
  - history-9
  - ancient-rome
---

* In some ways constitution resembled a Greek _politeia_
    * Had an assembly, in which all citizens voted
    * Had a council called senate which advising magistrates
    * Had magistrates who led army, proposed legislation, judged legal cases
* Political system was more aristocratic and hierarchical than [Athens](athens-democracy)
* Social relations were dominated by system called _patronage_ or _clientela_
    * People of unequal rank were bound by ties of loyalty and gratitude
* Ancient accounts contained struggle between _patricians_ and _plebeians_
* Plebeians forced patricians to publish legal code called _Twelve Tables_
