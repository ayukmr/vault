* Arab conquests outside Arabia began under second caliph, Umar
* Umayyad caliphs presided over Arab realm rather than religious empire
    * Ruled with armies consisting almost entirely of Muslim Arabs
    * Caliphs gradually replaced non-Muslims and introduced Arabic
    * Umayyad dynasty fell in 750 after decade of growing unrest
* [Umayyad Spain](umayyad-spain) developed distinctive Islamic culture due to separation
* In 750, last Umayyad caliph was overthrown, establishing [Abbasid Caliphate](abbasid-caliphate)
