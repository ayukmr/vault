tags:

* Told followers that sea would part before him
* Crusaders split into two groups, one led by Nicholas himself
    * Sea failed to part for Nicholas, so they went to Rome
    * Met with Pope Innocent, who told him to go home
    * Other group had similar fate, traveling to Italy
    * Both groups had some members that made their way to Germany
