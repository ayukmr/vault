title: Crusades
tags:
  - history-9
  - crusades
---

* Occurred between late 11th and late 13th centuries
* United groups of European kingdoms against common enemy
* Composed of upper and lower class people
* Motivations varied: political, religious, monetary, other
* Usually organized by the Pope or desired permission from them
    * Pope Urban II organized the [first crusade](first-crusade)
* Had an explicit religious orientation
* Were hugely expensive and required fundraising
* Often failed or were curtailed because of logistic problems
* Did not leave immediately, set up crusader states
    * Had markets in which [Italian city states](italian-city-states) traded in
