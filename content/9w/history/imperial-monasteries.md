title: Imperial Monasteries
tags:
  - history-9
  - tang-buddhism
---

* Staged ceremonies for welfare of state
* Invited laymen to participate in large vegetarian feasts
* Conducted collective mortuary and memorial rites for soldiers
* Provided lodgings to people traveling with state sponsorship
* Many turned provision of rooms into business
* Many became retreats where officials or other elite took temporary refuge
* Journeys into mountains to stay at temples became standard theme of Tang poetry
* Monasteries differed little from other landlords in many ways
