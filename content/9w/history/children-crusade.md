* Mythology and exaggeration surround events of crusade
* Made strong impression upon contemporary historians
* Once reaching age of seven, expected to start earning a living
* In agricultural families, expected to start working in fields
* Higher up social ladder, often sent to knights for skills
* Around a similar time, [Stephen](stephen-crusade) and [Nicholas](nicholas-crusade) both led crusades
