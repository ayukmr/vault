* Born in 1451 in [Genoa](italian-city-states), an important trade and crusader port city
* Speculated that conversion of great Asian ruler would allow for conquering Middle East
* First saw a Muslim in real life as a teenager, but read about Islam before
* Described many of the native people in the New World in Muslim world terms
* On August 3, 1492, set sail from Spain to find all-water route to Asia
* Landed on an island in the Bahamas and explored Caribbean for next five months
* In letter, declares that found land could easily be conquered by Spain
* Gave gifts in an effort to win affection and convert the people to Christians
