---
title: Xiongnu Nomads
tags:
  - history-9
  - qin-and-han
---

* Were united under leader, Modun, at around time of Qin unification
* As Modun united more and more nomadic tribes, he frequently led successful raids
* Remained a loose confederation of tribes opposed to a centralized state
* Gradually splintered into several groups, including one that allied with Han
* Some gifts received found their way to Afghanistan, India, Persia, eventually Rome
