tags:

* Iron permitted development of more lethal weapons
* Rulers eventually began drafting peasants as foot soldiers
* Many rulers lost faith in old beliefs of the Shang and early Zhou
* Became apparent that military power was more important than ancestral spirits
* Variety of [Chinese thinkers](warring-states-thinkers) debated central questions of the day
