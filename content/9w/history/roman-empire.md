* Did not begin writing own history until 200 BCE
    * Much of tradition is based on oral history
* Unlike Greek figures, historical characters were exceptional only in virtue
* Best-preserved account is from Livy, who lived from 59 BCE to 17 AD
* Had a profound sense of their origins and destiny
* Every year, posted record of important events called _Annales maximi_
* Sense of themselves was complicated because boundaries were very fluid
* Did not have to be born a Roman, extended citizenship to others
* Beginning around 650 BCE, began to develop into city with [constitution](roman-constitution)
