* Vast curtain of grassland from Hungary to Manchuria
* Historical significance derived from use of horses
* People of the Yamnaya culture were first to take advantage
    * Starting from around 3000 BCE, used horses and wheeled carts
    * Geneticists have found second cousins buried almost 900 miles apart
    * Descendants were in Europe, India, Near East, western China
* By seventh century BCE, Scythians occupied western end
* Around 200 BCE, [Xiongnu](xiongnu-nomads) occupied eastern end and collected payment from Han
