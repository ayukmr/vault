* Divide in Islam religion after Muhammad's death into Sunni and [Shia](shia-islam)
* Sunni believe there are many legal caliphs after Muhammad
    * Abu Bakr was legitimate, there is no concern about bloodline
* First four caliphs were known as Rashidun, meaning "rightly guided caliphs"
* Created class of scholars known as _ulama_ who created code of conduct
* Ruling was based on [caliphates](caliphates) controlled by different Muslim leaders
* First Christian Crusaders reached Holy Land and captured Jerusalem in 1099
    * Crusades had little lasting impact on the Islamic lands
* Muslims unified to face European enemy in mid-twelfth century
    * In 1187, Saladin recaptured Jerusalem from Europeans
    * In 1250, Turkish mamluk troops seized control and ended dynasty
