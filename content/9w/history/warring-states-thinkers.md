* Was the period in which [Legalism](legalism) school of thought was created
* [Confucius](confucius), creator of Confucianism, was born in 551BCE
* Group formed around Mozi, a thinker who lived between Confucius and Mencius
    * Argued Confucians were wasteful when honoring the dead
    * Argued they owed people bare necessities instead
* Group formed around [Daoism](daoism) posed challenge to Confucianism and Legalism
* Political knowledge was both precious and dangerous in the period
    * Han Feizi was poisoned when visiting Qin because of his knowledge
* Legalists wiped out all rivals to Qin state in 221 BCE
    * Triumph resulted in ensured survival of texts from most schools of thought
