* New converts turned to conquering neighboring territories
    * By 650, absorbed Palestine, Syria, Iraq, Iran and Egypt
* By 711, conquered North Africa and crossed into Christian Spain
* In 732, spread into Europe halted in France by Charles the Hammer
* Failed to conquer Constantinople twice, in 673 and 718
* Divisions started appearing within Islamic religion itself as [Shia](shia-islam)
* Sunni Islam moved away from Arabia, founding city of Baghdad in 750
* When Jerusalem fell to Muslims in 638, church suffered no harm
