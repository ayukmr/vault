* 14th century traveler, born and died in present day [Morocco](north-africa-islam)
* Wrote down travel in book called _The Rihla_, meaning the journey
* Often considered most well traveled person in pre-modern era
* Born into family of Islamic Scholar, acted as judge through travels
* Journey started with Hajj at 21, did not end for next 25 years
  * Visited almost all Muslim places in world, along with non-Muslim places
  * Traveled to [Kilwa](ibn-battuta-kilwa), Island off of coast of east Swahili coast
* Book was translated in 19th century, attracted scholarly attention
