* Muhammad's had spiritual method of interacting with divine
  * In Sunni Islam, Muhammad delivered God's message through prophetic voice
* Large body of literature developed to discuss practice
  * In Sunni Islam, Muhammad created sacred text that needs to be followed
* Each practitioner has their own relationship with divine
* Focus on encountering divine in this life, rather than in heaven
  * In Sunni Islam, people encounter the divine after death
* Use concept of _Tariqah_, meaning path/journey, inner journey toward divine
* More common in countryside due to caliphs enforcing certain practices
