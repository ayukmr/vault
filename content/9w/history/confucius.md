* Called on rulers to return to early Zhou principles
* Saw rituals and sacrifices as needed to respect the dead
* Argued basic human quality is capacity to empathize with others
* Noted that people learn most from watching and emulating
* Believed that virtue was powerful and contagious
* Never rose very high in any government and died in 479 BCE
* [Mengzhi](mengzhi), known to West as Mencius, expanded on his idealism
