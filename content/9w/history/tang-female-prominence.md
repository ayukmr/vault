* Female power was culmination of process that began with fall of Han
* Nomad warriors who occupied north China brought greater equality of men and women
* Women handled legal disputes, associated with politically powerful, entered government offices
* Emperors' wives often acted as political advisors to their husbands
* Tang was noted for prominence of many princesses who participated in [politics](tang-princess-politics)
