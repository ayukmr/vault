* Empress Wu, Taiping Princess, and Empress Wei had control for more than half a century
    * Tang as age of [female prominence](tang-female-prominence) arises in large part due to this
* Princesses helped seal political alliances through giving them in marriage
* Relative autonomy of Tang women is demonstrated in manuals of female conduct
* While Tang was time of freedom and power for elite women, others became economic commodities
* Distinguishing wives from concubines was important both to marriage and social hierarchy
