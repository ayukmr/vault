* Addressed issues not dealt with in the Quran
    * Became indispensable tool in formation of Islamic law
* In earliest stages, were muddled and unregulated
* In less than two centuries after Muhammad's death, 700 were being circulated
    * Great majority were fabricated to legitimize personal beliefs
* After few generations, anything connected to Muhammad was given status of hadith
* In ninth and tenth centuries, effort was made to separate reliable hadith
