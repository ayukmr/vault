* Most successful and influential school was Legalist School (_fajia_)
* Legalist doctrines developed in several states over several centuries
* Legalists guided and responded to tech and org innovations
* While others attacked, Legalists promoted changes to end Warring States period
* Variety of Legalists teachings were synthesized into the _Book of Han Feizi_
    * Argued past was dead, buried and irrelevant to present
    * Argued human nature is evil, pleasure and threats are only motivators
