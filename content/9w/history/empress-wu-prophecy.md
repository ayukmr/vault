* Cited scripture saying Maitreya would descend into magical city
    * Provided model for her own Bright Hall complex at Luoyang
* She sponsored translation of _Great Cloud Sutra_ with commentary
* In 690, translation was distributed to all monasteries of empire
* Five years later, she added the name Maitreya to her own title
