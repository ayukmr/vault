* Plays critical role in maintaining law and order
* Uses tools including taxes and regulations to reach everyone
* Represents concerns of its citizens through politics
* Every government can fail in different ways
    * People in charge conflict with people carrying out tasks
    * Prioritization of short-term results to gain popularity
    * Corruption with public servants and politicians misusing power
* Regulation often struggles to keep up with changing needs
