---
title: Radiative Forcing
tags:
  - climate-science
---

* Amount by which factor changes radiation balance
* Positive means more energy is going in than out
* Measured in watts per meter squared, $W/m^2$
* System feedback loops are taken into account
    * Positive increases, negative neutralizes
* Greenhouse gas emissions have large forcing values
* Climate sensitivity is warming caused by given forcing
    * Typically amount of warming for doubling of CO2
    * Most common value estimate is around 3 celsius
