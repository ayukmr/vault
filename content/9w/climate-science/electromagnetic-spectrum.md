tags:

* Wavelength measured in nanometers, 1e-9 meters
    * Represented as lambda in equations
* Frequency is amount of waves per second
    * Frequency and wavelength are inversely related
    * Frequency and energy are directly related
* Shortwave radiation is mostly visible light
* Longwave radiation is mostly infrared light
