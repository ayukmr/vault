---
title: Capabilities Approach
tags:
  - climate-science
---

* Developed by Indian economist and Nobel Laureate Amartya Sen
* Focuses on ability of individuals to choose their own values
* American philosopher Martha Nussbaum built on this by specifying values
    * Includes bodily health, integrity, emotions, senses, imagination, thought
* Fundamentally about expanding opportunity to live fulfilling life
* Often focused on deprivations where individuals cannot access values
