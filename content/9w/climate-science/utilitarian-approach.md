---
title: Utilitarian Approach
tags:
  - climate-science
---

* Originally formulated by English philosopher Jeremy Bentham
* Introduces concept of "utility", measure of usefulness or happiness
    * Goal of society is to maximize total utility across members
* Modern economics took idea of utility and transformed approach
    * Individuals allocate resources to maximize their utility
* Idea is the basis for primary focus on incomes to acquire utility
* Deprivation leads to a focus on defining "poverty" as lack of income
