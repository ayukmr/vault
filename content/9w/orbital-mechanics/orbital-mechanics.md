* Uses [calculus](calculus) extensively for various calculations
* Most difficult part for rockets is [escaping atmosphere](escape-velocity)
* Perigee and apogee are lowest and highest point in orbit respectively
* 464 m/s is given for free when taking off east from equator
* Bodies orbit around center of mass of both bodies
* There are 5 [equilibrium points](lagrange-points) for 3rd body in 3 body system
