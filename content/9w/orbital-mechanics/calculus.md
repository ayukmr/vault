## Simple
* Uses distance, speed, acceleration

## More Complex
* Non-constant change
* Decelerating from 25 m/s to 0 m/s over 5 secs
    * `25m/s * 5s / 2 = 62.5m`
* With graph of time, integration is area under line
* With graph of measurement, differentiation is slope of line

## Symbolic vs Empirical
* Empirical is simpler, but requires lots of repetitive calculations
* Symbolic is more complex, but produces simpler equations
