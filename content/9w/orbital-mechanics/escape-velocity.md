---
title: Escape Velocity
tags:
  - orbital-mechanics
---

* When gravity cannot pull body back from apogee, orbit becomes parabola or hyperbola
* Speed required to break out of orbit is escape velocity
    * 11 km/s to escape earth from surface
    * 1324 km/s to escape sun from earth's orbit
    * 20000 km/s to escape Milky Way from sun's orbit
