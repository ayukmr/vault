## Elements to Avoid
* Iconography / symbolism
* Commission / patron
* Political / social / economic context

## Elements to Look For
* Scale
    * Where we stand in relation to painting
    * Standing back, coming close

* Composition
    * Arrangement of forms
    * Background, foreground
    * Framing with landscape
    * Framing by trees, vertical architecture

* Pictorial space
    * The illusion of 3D space
    * Overlapping of objects
    * Atmospheric and linear perspective

* Form
    * Representation of solids in space
    * Curvilinear, rectilinear shapes
    * Some feel very solid, some are delicate

* Line
    * Defines form or edge

* Color
    * Contrast, blues versus brown earth

* Light
    * Illumination, shadow
    * Moving tones from light into dark
    * Chiaroscuro

* Tone
    * Lightness or darkness of color

* Texture
    * The visual tactility of surfaces

* Pattern
    * Repetition of decorative design
    * Organic patterns (leaf forms, grass forms)
    * Often denies pictorial space
