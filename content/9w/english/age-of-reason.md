* Also known as Enlightenment, happened around 1700s
* Emphasized reasoning over feelings
    * Had philosophical thinking and tradition
* Prompted the industrial revolution from 1760-1840
    * Valued greater efficiency at cost to people
* [Romanticism period](romanticism) happened after the Age of Reason
