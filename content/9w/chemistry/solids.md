## Ionic
* Examples: $NaCl$, $CaCO^3$
* Conductive when dissociated in water
* High melting point, brittle

## Covalent
* Examples: Diamond
* Great insulators
* Extremely hard
* Extremely high melting point

## Molecular
* Examples: Sucrose, candle wax
* Not conductive when dissociated in water
* Low melting point, relatively brittle

## Metallic
* Examples: Gold, Copper, Aluminum
* Conductive, transfers heat and electricity
* High melting point, ductile
