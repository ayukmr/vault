* Least electronegative atom is placed at center
* Group numbers of elements combined is amount of electrons
* Uses single, double, triple bonds to reach electron count

## Amounts
* Hydrogen requires 2 electrons using 1 bond
* Boron and Aluminum requires 6 electrons using 3 bonds
* Carbon, Nitrogen, Oxygen, Fluorine, Neon require 8 electrons
* 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 periods require 8 electrons, can have more
