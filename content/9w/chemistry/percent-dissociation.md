* $\% dissociation = \frac{[H3O+]}{[HA]}$
* [Equilibrium constant](equilibrium-constant) determines results

## Example
* $HA + H_2O \rightleftharpoons A^- + H_3O^+$

|             | $HA$     | $H_2O$    | $A^-$     | $H_3O^+$     |
|-------------|--------|--------|--------|----------|
| Initial     | 1.0 M  | Excess | 0 M    | 0 M      |
| Change      | -0.2 M | N/A    | +0.2 M | +0.2 M   |
| Equilibrium | 0.8 M  | N/A    | 0.2 M  | 0.2 M    |

* $\% dissociation = \frac{0.2 M}{1.0 M} = 20\%$
