title: Neutralization Reactions
tags:
  - chemistry-9
---

* $HA (A) + H_2O (B) \rightleftharpoons H_3O^+ (CA) + A^- (CB)$
    * $HA$ is generic weak acid
    * First $H$ in $HA$ is ionizable (acidic) proton
* $NH_3 (B) + HF (A) \rightleftharpoons NH_4^+ (CA) + F^- (CB)$
* $NH_4^+ (A) + H_2O (B) \rightleftharpoons NH_3 (CB) + H_3O^- (CA)$
* $PO_4^{3-} (B) + HC_2H_3O_2 (A) \rightleftharpoons HPO_4^{2-} (CA) + C_2H_3O_2^- (CB)$
    * First $H$ in $HC_2H_3O_2$ is ionizable proton
* $C_2O_4^{2-} (B) + H_2O (A) \rightleftharpoons HC_2O_4^- (CA) + OH (CB)$
* $HC_2H_3O_2 (A) + H_2O (B) \rightleftharpoons H_3O^+ (CA) + C_2H_3O_2^- (CB)$
