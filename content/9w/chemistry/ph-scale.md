* Acid when $pH < 7$, has more $H_3O^+$
* Base when $pH > 7$, has more $OH^-$
* Neutral when $pH = 7$, has equal amounts
* Acids donate proton, bases receive proton
* Lone pairs make a molecule basic
* Strong acids and bases fully dissociate in $H_2O$
    * Higher [% dissociation](percent-dissociation) is stronger
* $H_2O$ is amphoteric, can act as acid or base
* [Certain reactions](neutralization-reactions) are reversible if in equilibrium

## Equations
* $pH = -log[H_3O^+]$, $pOH = -log[OH^-]$
* $H_3O^+ = 10^{-pH}$, $OH^- = 10^{-pOH}$
* $[H_3O^+] \cdot [OH^-] = 10^{-14}$, $pH + pOH = 14$


## Example
* $HC_2H_3O_2 + H_2O \rightleftharpoons H_3O^+ + C_2H_3O_2^-$

|             | $HC_2H_3O_2$      | $H_2O$    | $H_3O^+$     | $C_2H_3O_2$      |
|-------------|--------------|--------|----------|-------------|
| Initial     | 1.0 M        | Excess | 0 M      | 0 M         |
| Change      | -x M         | N/A    | +x M     | +x M        |
| Equilibrium | 1.2 - x M    | N/A    | x M      | x M         |

* $K_a = 1.45 \cdot 10^{-5} = \frac{x^2}{1.2 - x}$
* $x_1 = 4.1 \cdot 10^{-3}$
