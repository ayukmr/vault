# causality and constellations

* direction of causality is unclear
    * harder to research economics compared to placebos etc.
    * certain moments highlighted as clear examples
        * gold discoveries of 1897/1914
        * during/after WWI and WWII
* "_self fulfilling prophecy_"
    * sunspots -> extraneous noise believed to cause impact
* contagious stories
    * fisher "permanently high plateau"
* stories in driving human activity
    * thinking of economy as "sick" or "healthy"
        * progress of an economy is not "one-dimensional" or one-sided
* flashbulb memory
    * especially poignant long term memory moments
        * i.e. pearl harbor, 9/11
    * 9/11 actually improved economy, economists dismissed flashbulb quality at the time
        * call to action from president bush addressed at "helping" nation
* fake news
    * brain processes for "reality monitoring" are imperfect
    * _kayfabe_ in wrestling -> people enjoy believing in a story and don't care about facts
    * fakery causes economic outcomes, not in reverse
