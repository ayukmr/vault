# causal loop diagrams

* links have (+)/(-) polarity, loops are positive (reinforcing)/negative (balancing)
    * S (same)/O (opposite) sometimes used instead of +/-
* stocks (accumulation of resources)/flows (rates of change that alter stocks) treated the same
* links _must_ be causal rather than correlative
* loop is positive with even # of negative links, negative with odd # of negative links
    * polarity is known as the "open loop gain" of the loop
* indicate delays between nodes and their effects, are often responsible between short-/long-run effects
