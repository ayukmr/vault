# Wars at the Water's Edge

* Indigenous had power over coasts
* In many cases, colonizers were kept isolated and had to retreat
* In 1620, Mayflower brought 102 colonists who landed
    * Disease outbreak killed almost 90% of Wampanoags
    * Heavily reduced Wampanoags took in newcomers
    * Allowed English to build colony since they were 'useful'
    * Was clear English could do little without indigenous
* Colonists had low views of indigenous, thought land was not utilized
* Plymouth remained sole colony until 1629
* Imperial rivalries exacerbated situation of colonies
    * Dutch intimidated English, caused race for clamshells
* Pequot War marked shift from maritime colonialism to intrusive territorial
