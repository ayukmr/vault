## [The Mexican War and the Expansion of Slavery](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KkgkZwQ9HgQ&list=PLSuwqsAnJMtyg3ROpOADVoW1NV5kmskFM&index=30)

* slavery entered national politics to stay in mid to late 1840s-1850s
* not in form of abolitionist, thought of in western expansion of new territories
* us annexed texas in 1845, led to acquisition of new territory in 1848
    * what was going to be status of slavery in this area?
* some historians date start of civil war in 1845
* wilmot proviso in 1846, slavery would not be allowed in territory acquired from mexico
* abolitionists not bothered by expansion; cared more about current states
* expansion of slavery was an 'available issue' constitutionally
    * widely shared belief that congress had no control over slavery; was at state level
    * nothing congress could do with existing slavery, but constitution allows congress to dictate slavery in new territories
* growing fear in the south of becoming permanent, shrinking minority
* commonly thought that slave system required expansion to survive
* free soil, free of slavery; free soil was linked with racism, distinct from abolitionism
    * anti-slavery has no views on future of black people, did not want free black people either
    * appeals to white northerners with self-interest
    * erases the question of equity from debate over slavery

## [The Politics of Slavery](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeCfEe__Q4g&list=PLSuwqsAnJMtyg3ROpOADVoW1NV5kmskFM&index=32)

* in 1840s, two positions emerged
    * calhoun put forward that territories are commonly held by states, people of all states must have equal rights to bringing property into territories
    * popular sovereignty, people of the territory should decide rather than congress
        * takes slavery out of national politics
        * became the official position of democratic party
        * both northerners and southerners agreed on stance
* both whigs and democrats have strong north/south wings
    * both have interests in keeping slavery out of national politics
    * in the south, whigs/democrats both desire to keep slavery
    * ~~in the north, whigs/democrats tend to not bring up slavery~~
* abolitionists created liberty party in order to raise the issue
* northern whigs/democrats had anti-slavery factions, resented southern control of government
* democrats resented president polk, thought he was pulling party into pro-southern direction
* anti-slavery democrats, anti-slavery whigs, liberty party created free soil party
* free soil added homestead provision, that government should grant free land to western settlers
* anti-slavery becomes interlinked with economics as northeners saw opportunity in homestead

## [The Dred Scott Case](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRaA0GQgobI&list=PLSuwqsAnJMtyg3ROpOADVoW1NV5kmskFM&index=59)

* most important event of buchanan's administration happened near end
* slaveholder brought dred scott into free states of illinois, wisconsin
* dred scott sued in 1846 in missouri court for freedom
    * "somerset principle," residence in free area granted freedom
* local court agreed, on appeal missouri court reversed decision in 1852
    * for purely political reason, preventing aid to abolitionists
* case was argued by supreme court in 1856
* taney believed in colonization; thought free slaves should be sent to liberia
