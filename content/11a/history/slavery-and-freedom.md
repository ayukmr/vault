# Slavery and Freedom: The American Paradox

* Historians interested in liberty, democracy need to consider slavery
    * Slaves made up one fifth of population, not an exception
    * Also cannot argue that slavery is primary and liberty is secondary
* Rise of liberty and equality was accompanied by rise of slavery
    * Contradictory developments took place simultaneously
* "To a very large degree it may be said that Americans bought their independence with slave labor."
* Despite pride of liberty, England produced 4/5 free, 1/5 slaves population
* Jefferson saw claims of freedom without independence as overturning liberty
* Locke did not extend rights of liberty to landless poor
    * Had scheme of compulsory labor by having children work from age of 3
    * Freed time for mothers, causing them to also work
* Fletcher promoted both liberty and continuation of slavery
    * Argued Scotland was burdened with 200k "idle rogues" who could be slaves
* By 1630s, colony of Virgina seemed to be working out
    * Took in people of all countries and "made Englishmen out of them"
    * Most Africans came as slaves, substantial amount became free
        * All of them had similar rights and duties as other Virginians
* "Virginia, in other words, was absorbing England's surplus laborers mainly by killing them."
    * As mortality decreased, growing class of Virginians in poverty who were free
    * Had social problem similar to the one of England
        * Colony began to restrict liberties of those without land
* African slaves allowed for having separate laws for white versus black
