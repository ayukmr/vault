* Who, what, when, where, why?
    * Freeman Hunt, edited _Hunt's Merchants' Magazine_
    * 1856 in Quincy, Massachusetts
        * Compiled number of short pieces in _Worth and Wealth_
    * Provided reassurance and promotion of the businessman
* What does the source say?
    * General pieces of advice for businessmen
        * Courageous, "able commander"
        * Patience, imagination, planning
            * General opportunism
    * Economy
        * Having connection to industry
        * Being 'economical' in usage of money
        * Adhering to a certain business
        * Managing pursuits of money
* What connections can you draw to the YAWP chapter you read?
    * In a new system heavily defined by the divide between "wealthy businessmen and impoverished wage laborers," the ethical and logistical restraints given by Hunt show an attempt to create an even larger divide in terms of sophistication
* What does the source help us understand about this period in U.S. history?
    * The expectations of rising in the new class structure; though I personally think that most of Hunt's advice is sound in general
* What interpretive and historical questions does the source raise?
    * Would there be any similar guides for moving from an 'impoverished wage laborer' to a 'wealthy businessman', given that there is an insurmountable gap between the groups?
