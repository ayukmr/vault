## bailyn

* arguing trying to reclaim liberty through revolution
* rather than fearmongering, focused on sermons etc
    * trying to reason with others
* liberty is humanity's "original dignity"
* british types of power
    * political power - ability to implement power
    * military power - largest navy in the world
* handed out pamphlets, trying to reason with people
    * explained lack of liberty was also in africa, europe
    * inspired hope for change
* people listening to sermons were unaware they were being influenced

## breen

* colonial indulgence caused british imports
    * "yay being 'british'"
    * 1740s, consumer revolution
* taxations brings reevaluation of colonial habits of consumption
    * follows seven years war
* piling economic pressure, growing indebtedness
    * economic depression
* stamp act - solidifies illegitimacy of shared political identity as British
* boycotts
    * supporters were seen as "sacrificing" for revolution
    * non-supporters were shamed
* goal was always to separate from british because of unfair treatment
* system of protests is forgotten in other historian accounts
    * much more prominence is put into constitution itself, rather than factors behind it

## linebaugh & rediker

* motley crew, multiethnic group of sailors, slaves, laborers
    * lead movement from below
    * shaped social, organizational, structural hierarchies
* revolution was not solely elite-led
    * argue that was possible through multiracial, working-class resistance
* gradual decreasing of colonial power
* many-headed hydra metaphor
    * many heads
        * attacking from different angles (water + land, burning + hostages, sailors + slaves)
        * different ways of pressuring government
    * hydra
        * one head gets cut off, another grows
        * one revolution fails, another begins
    * strike in liverpool shows example
* emphasizes importance of motley crew
    * america wouldn't exist without enslaved people, "lower class"
