* citizens hold $25\%$ disposable income in cash
* banks hold $10\%$ of deposits on reserve with central bank

5a/
* given $i = 5\%$
* $Y = Z$
* $= C + I + G$
* $= 1 + 0.5(Y - T) + 1 - 0.5i + 0.275$
* $= 1 + 0.5(Y - 0.2Y) - 0.5i + 0.275$
* $Y - 0.5(Y - 0.2Y) = 1 + 1 - 0.5i + 0.275$
* $0.6Y = 2.275 - 0.5i$
* $Y = \frac{2.25}{0.6} = 3.75$

5b/ 
* given $i = 5\%$
* $M_d = (Y - T)(0.3 - i)$
* $= (Y - 0.2Y)(0.3 - i)$
* $= 0.75$
* $M = M_d = 0.75$

5c/
