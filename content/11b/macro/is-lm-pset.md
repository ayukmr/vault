# is-lm problem set

1/ How did covid affect interest rates and output? How did the government response(s) then 
affect interest rates and output?

a/

* $C \downarrow$ -> consumption falls ($IS$ left)
* $M^d \downarrow$ -> money demand falls ($LM$ down)

* output decreases with lower $C$
* $i$ equilibrium decreases based on curves moving

![[is-lm-pset-1.png|400]]

b/

* government response
    * $G \uparrow$ -> government spending increases for stimulus etc ($IS$ right)
    * $M^s \uparrow$ -> stimulate demand helping economy ($LM$ down)

* output increases with increased $G$
* $i$ seems to stay the same in new equilibrium

![[is-lm-pset-2.png|400]]

2/ How would a wave of immigration affect interest rates and output?

* $Y \uparrow$ -> immigrants increase economy output ($IS$ right)
* $T \uparrow$ -> taxation rises with more immigrants, less than $Y \uparrow$ though ($IS$ left)
* $M^d \uparrow$ -> demand for money increases with more people ($LM$ up)

* output increases with increased $Y$
* $i$ increases in new equilibrium

![[is-lm-pset-3.png|400]]

3/ What effect would a sudden spike in automation have on interest rates and output?

* $Y \uparrow$ -> output suddenly increases ($IS$ right)
* $C \uparrow$ -> consumption would likely increase with more goods ($IS$ right)

![[is-lm-pset-4.png|400]]

* output increases with $Y \uparrow$ and $C \uparrow$
* interest increases with $IS$ moving

4/ How would alien contact affect interest rates and output?

* (assuming the aliens possess deadly weapons)
* $Y \downarrow$ -> output falls as people desire spending their life on non-work stuff ($IS$ left)
* $C \uparrow$ -> people spend money for prep, but cannot be sustained so less than $Y \downarrow$ ($IS$ right)
* $M^d$ -> people attempt to get more secure liquid money ($LM$ up)

* output falls as $Y$ goes down
* interest rate seems relatively the same

![[is-lm-pset-5.png|400]]

5/ What is something besides a change in monetary policy or banking laws that could shift the LM curve?

* changing the price level $P$ would shift the $LM$ curve, since the curve is based on $\frac{M}{P}$ instead of just $M$ alone
