# conspicuous consumption

## ctx

* idea that purchases, interests of wealth were intended to demonstrate superiority

## source

* consumption of better grades of good is restricted to 'leisure class'  
    * tends to 'disappear' under industrial system based on wage labour
* upper class has to curate their 'taste' in goods
* conspicuous consumption of goods is a 'means of reputability to the gentlemen of leisure'
* idea of non-instrumentality to bought goods; e.g. a fast horse, wasteful other than racing
