# army of unemployed

## ctx

* growth of factory system left working class unsettled
    * taylorism reduces power of individual laborers
* unionization—knights of labors, founded in 1869
* membership reached 700k by 1885, though did not win any significant victories

## source

* cincinnati riots by knights were not caused by ire of labor leader; rather, demand was for removal of unjust technicalities, delays, discriminations etc
* 'army of the discontented' continues to amass forces, could one day arise in its wrath
* pressuring 'men who taunt others with unequal earnings, and distort the truth'
    * accuses vanderbilt of lying—"I know of no business in the United States, in which a skilled mechanic ... gets money enough for ... a barrel of flour"
* further argues that child labor wages should be the same; can operate machines just as well as an adult
* ends with a assertion that less time will lead to laborers understanding the true inequality
