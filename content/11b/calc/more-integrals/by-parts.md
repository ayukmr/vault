# integration by parts

start with $\int (f \cdot g)' dx$
using product rule for derivatives, $= \int (f' \cdot g)dx + \int (f \cdot g') dx$

left side simplifies to $f \cdot g$

isolate one derivative with $\int f \cdot g' dx = f \cdot g - \int f' \cdot gdx$

which gives $\int u \cdot v' dx = uv - \int u' \cdot v dx$
where $u = f$, $v = g$
